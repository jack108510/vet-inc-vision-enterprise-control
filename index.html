Platform

What the platform manages

Each capability is built for the operators, finance leads, and regional managers who own pricing decisions in a multi-site group—not to replace them.

Authorization workflow

Changes require explicit stakeholder sign-off before they can advance. Approvals are attributed and time-stamped so the record is clear.

PMS catalogue mapping

Approved items are matched to the correct catalogue entries across your practice management systems and clinic locations.

Exception management

Locations or items that deviate from the approved rollout are flagged and held for review before proceeding.

Rollout status

Track where each approved change stands across every clinic in your group—from authorization through to confirmed implementation.

Audit history

A complete, structured record of every authorization, mapping decision, and implementation event, accessible for review at any time.

Controlled PMS writeback

Production writeback to your PMS is executed only after technical validation and contractual approval on a per-group basis.

Planned capability

Integration scope

Deliberate implementation, not autonomous action

Vet INC is designed to support the people responsible for pricing decisions in a veterinary group. It enforces a structured process; it does not make decisions independently.

On integration and writeback

Direct production PMS writeback is in development and will be deployed only after technical validation and contractual approval for each group. Current implementations operate through structured authorization and review workflows. Live PMS connectors and autonomous price changes are not currently available—they are a planned and contractually governed future capability.

FeeWatch for independent practices

FeeWatch

FeeWatch adds historical context to a clinic’s own pricing-review cadence by studying its sales, revenue, and visit records. It does not set prices, generate automatic recommendations, or guarantee outcomes—it gives your team better information for decisions you make on your schedule.
